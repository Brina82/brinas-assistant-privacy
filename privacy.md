Privacy Policy — Brina’s assistant

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Brina’s assistant is a Discord bot operated by Brina82 and designed to provide utility and entertainment features within Discord servers.

Information We Process

When you interact with Brina’s assistant, the bot may process information provided through Discord messages in order to recognize and respond to supported commands.

The bot may also store the following information:

* Your Discord User ID
* Your selected timezone preference, if you use the timezone feature

The Discord User ID is used to associate your saved preferences with your Discord account.

Message Content

Brina’s assistant processes message content when necessary to identify and execute its supported commands.

Message content is not intentionally stored as a permanent database record and is not sold, shared for advertising purposes, or used to build user profiles.

Message content is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Temporary Information

Some features may temporarily process information while a request is being handled. For example, AFK and ping-related functionality may use temporary information during operation. Such information is not intended to be permanently stored.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share stored user information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by Discord as part of providing the Discord platform and its services. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to information processed by Discord.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved timezone preferences are retained until they are reset or otherwise removed by the bot’s functionality, or until the associated data is deleted by the bot operator.

If you would like your stored timezone preference and associated Discord User ID removed, you may contact the bot operator, or use the appropriate command to remove it automatically. (;tz reset)

Children’s Privacy

Brina’s assistant is a Discord bot and does not knowingly collect additional personal information from users beyond what is described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this page.

Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or data stored by Brina’s assistant, please contact the bot operator through the Discord server where the bot is used.
